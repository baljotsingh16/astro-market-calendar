About this calendar

Designed for Android phones including Samsung Galaxy S24 Ultra. Calendar times use IST. NSE cash-session reference: 09:15–15:30. Degree dates are calendar calculations, not astronomical ephemeris events.

Sources used include AstroSage, Prokerala and Indian Panchang/transit calendars. The starter data is a planning aid, not a verified Mumbai ephemeris. Confirm exact tithi, nakshatra, Bhadra and planetary timings against a Mumbai/India-local Panchang or ephemeris. Event markers are date reminders, not evidence that markets will reverse.
